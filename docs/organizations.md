# Organizations

Zoop envisions supporting large-scale, multi-tenant groupings.

*Note: This is a conceptual overview to avoid prematurely defining business logic.*

## Conceptual Groupings

Zoop could eventually support structured organizational boundaries:

- **Universities**: Campus-wide sharing, authenticated via student identities.
- **Communities**: Grassroots neighborhood internet sharing collectives.
- **Institutions**: Corporate or non-profit managed networks.
- **Managed Networks**: ISP-like deployments utilizing Zoop architecture for connectivity and roaming.

Organizations would provide policy boundaries, aggregate billing, and centralized administration for subsets of endpoints and accounts.
