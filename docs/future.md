# Future Ideas

This document preserves ideas that may become part of Zoop later, but are **not yet decided or implemented**.

- **Community Sharing**: Grassroots connectivity networks where neighbors provide each other with internet access.
- **Paid Sharing**: Mechanisms for providers to be financially compensated for the bandwidth they share.
- **Bandwidth Accounting**: Systems to track and measure the exact amount of bandwidth consumed by recipients and provided by providers.
- **Institutional Networks**: Deployments at large corporate campuses or organizations.
- **University Deployments**: Specific architectures tailored to students sharing access within and across dorms.
- **Routers**: Integration of the Zoop agent directly into consumer routers (e.g., OpenWrt) to provide connectivity without requiring client software on user devices.
- **Other future ideas**: To be discussed.
